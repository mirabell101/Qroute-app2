Show all routes at once

Clicking a stop/terminal immediately draws every route serving it. This avoids another choice, but overlapping lines and stops can clutter the map.

Recommendation: A keeps the map readable when locations have multiple routes, while still showing every stop automatically once a route is chosen. Either way, clicking a route itself can immediately show that route end-to-end.
