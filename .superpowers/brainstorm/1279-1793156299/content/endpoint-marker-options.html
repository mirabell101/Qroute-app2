Route stop selection and map icons

Choose the marker style for showing your boarding point and destination. Either choice keeps any stop usable in either direction.

BOARDING
DESTINATION

1. Vehicle icon with start/end colors

Train, bus, or jeepney glyph stays visible in both markers; green and red show which end is boarding and which is destination. Compact and easy to recognize.

START
END

2. Vehicle icon with explicit labels

Both endpoints use the transport type’s color, with small “Start” and “End” badges. Labels are unmistakable but take more map space.

Trip behavior in either style: click any route stop to board, click any different stop (earlier or later in the stored order) to set or change the destination, and fare uses the distance between the two stops.
